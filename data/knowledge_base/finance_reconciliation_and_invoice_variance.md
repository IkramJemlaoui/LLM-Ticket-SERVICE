# Finance Reconciliation and Invoice Variance

For differences between an invoice, purchase order, or approved payment amount, record the invoice reference, purchase-order reference, expected amount, actual amount, currency, supplier, and approval status. Finance & Procurement owns payment-control decisions. Do not release, change, or duplicate a payment until an authorised Finance reviewer confirms the variance.

When the disagreement concerns dashboard totals or metric definitions rather than a payable transaction, route the data investigation to Data & Analytics and keep Finance as the business owner of the approved definition.

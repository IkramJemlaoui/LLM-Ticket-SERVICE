# Cross-Department Request Routing

Use the owner of the affected service or requested work as the destination team. The department experiencing the impact may be different from the resolver. Route dashboards, datasets, pipelines, KPI definitions, and data reconciliation to Data & Analytics. Route access permissions to Identity & Access, checkout incidents to Ecommerce & Customer Experience, and process execution issues to the owning operational team.

When ownership is unclear, ask for the application, process, dataset, or service name. Never grant access or execute a consequential action from the ticket description alone.

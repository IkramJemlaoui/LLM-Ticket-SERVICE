# Order and Inventory Reconciliation

When orders are missing, duplicated, or inconsistent between commerce, warehouse, and reporting systems, capture a redacted order reference, time window, affected channel, warehouse or region, expected count, actual count, and last successful update. Separate customer checkout failures from downstream fulfilment or analytics delays.

Checkout and payment-state incidents belong to Ecommerce & Customer Experience. Duplicate fulfilment or warehouse execution issues belong to Operations & Fulfillment. Missing dashboard or dataset records belong to Data & Analytics. Do not manually recreate orders until duplicate-processing risk is reviewed.
